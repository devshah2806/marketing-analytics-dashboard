06 — Key Insights

What to act on
Scale Facebook Retargeting
Conversions_Retargeting is the second-best CPA ($5.95) with CTR of 4.63% — but only has 369K impressions. It's constrained by audience size. Expand to 2–3% lookalikes of converters to multiply volume without sacrificing efficiency.
Cut or Fix Google Generic Search
Search_Generic_Terms at $24.80 CPA ($15.5K spend) is the single biggest drag on account performance. Add negative keyword lists, tighten to exact/phrase match, and cap daily budget. Reallocate savings to Brand Terms or Shopping.
Monitor TikTok Awareness_GenZ
Awareness_GenZ has both the lowest video completion (22.2%) and highest CPA among TikTok campaigns ($13.00). The creative isn't landing. A/B test new hooks — first 3 seconds are losing viewers faster than other campaigns.

07 — Recommended Channel Strategy

Each platform has a job — use it accordingly
01
TikTok
Awareness & reach
Lowest CPM ($2.59), massive scale (28.7M impr.), strong video completion on Influencer content. Don't optimize for conversion here — optimize for completion rate and downstream retargeting pools.
02
Facebook
Retargeting & mid-funnel
Best platform CPA ($7.64). Retargeting campaign at $5.95 is the strongest converter in the account. Feed it with TikTok warm audiences. Expand lookalikes aggressively — audience is undersized relative to its efficiency.
03
Google
Capture intent & close
Brand Terms ($5.10) and Shopping ($6.34) are where intent converts. Concentrate budget here. Kill or restructure Generic Search. Google's job is to catch people already in the market — not to create demand.
